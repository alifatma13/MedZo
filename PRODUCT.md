# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Australian GPs, medical specialists, and practice owners/managers. They are time-poor clinicians who have clinical expertise but are frustrated by the operational side of running a practice. They access the site on desktop when researching practice management services; they are decision-makers evaluating whether to outsource admin, billing, or management.

## Product Purpose

MedZo is a medical practice management company. The site's job is to build trust with sceptical healthcare professionals, clearly communicate three services (virtual receptionist, managerial support, billing), and convert visitors into consult bookings. Success means a prospective client books a free consultation.

## Brand Personality

Assured, precise, human. Not corporate, not clinical-sterile. The brand sits at the intersection of healthcare credibility and warm professional service.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (feature grids, hero-metric templates, gradient text)
- Cold clinical aesthetics (all white, blue crosses, hospital vibes)
- Overcrowded agency sites with excessive animation and visual noise

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility through specificity** — every claim names something real (Medicare, AHPRA, Gentu) rather than using generic adjectives.
2. **Generous whitespace as a trust signal** — healthcare professionals read carefully; space lets copy breathe.
3. **Consistent rhythm** — sections feel like a considered document, not a collage of independently designed blocks.
4. **Motion serves, never distracts** — animations orient the user; they never compete with content.
5. **One clear action per section** — each section has one job; CTAs are never competing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reduced-motion support required on all animations. All images require meaningful alt text. Keyboard navigation must work across all interactive elements.
