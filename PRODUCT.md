# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Australian doctors (GPs, specialists, allied health practitioners) and practice managers who are technically capable but time-poor. They use this site while evaluating whether to outsource admin burden. Context: they are pulled away from patient care by billing, compliance, and scheduling. They scan rather than read; they need to reach trust quickly.

## Product Purpose

MedZo provides end-to-end medical practice management for Australian clinics: virtual receptionists, billing, compliance, scheduling, and operations. Success looks like a doctor booking a free consultation within two minutes of landing on the site.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, efficient, modern. No-nonsense and outcome-focused. Feels like a well-run clinic, not a consultancy or a startup. Speaks with authority, not warmth-for-its-own-sake.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS aesthetic: gradient-heavy, purple-toned, Intercom/HubSpot-style startup design.
- Old-school medical: clinical white, red-cross institutional, government health portal feel.
- Neither SaaS startup nor healthcare bureaucracy.

## Design Principles

1. **Outcome first.** Every section must make the value proposition immediately legible. No burying the point in preamble.
2. **Precision over decoration.** Intentional whitespace, clean geometry, no visual noise. The interface should feel as well-run as the practices MedZo manages.
3. **Authority without stiffness.** Professional enough that a consultant trusts it; modern enough that a tech-forward GP respects it.
4. **Doctors scan, not browse.** Information hierarchy is everything. Short sentences, clear labels, no padding copy.
5. **The system is the product.** Design should feel systematic and deliberate, not decorative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Keyboard-navigable, screen-reader-friendly. Reduced-motion respected on all animations (already enforced in CLAUDE.md).
